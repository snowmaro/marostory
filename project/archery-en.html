Framed Archer

3D archery · Mystery

Clear your name,
one arrow at a time.

Champion archer Sera has been framed for the king’s fall. Recreate six witnesses’ trick shots and find the real archer in this 3D archery game.

AndroidGoogle Play

Story

The king fell during the championship final, and beside the throne lay Sera’s red-feathered arrow.

Framed for a crime she did not commit, the champion archer breaks out of prison and travels the kingdom’s six regions to find six witnesses who each hold a piece of the truth.

Features

A story told
in arrows.

01

Meet six witnesses

Find the six people who each saw part of that day, and recreate their signature trick shots.

02

Pin clues to your case board

Every recreated shot earns a clue for your case board, one step closer to the real archer.

03

30 story challenges

Cut ropes, ring bells, snuff out lanterns, pop balloons and hit moving targets. Every level has its own goal.

04

Moments that feel great

Hit a 10 and follow it in the slow-motion arrow cam, or split an arrow with a rare Robin Hood shot.

05

Six lands to explore

A prison-tower meadow, a desert circus, a snow fortress, the castle at night, autumn hunting grounds and an old range.

How to play

Read the wind. Release.

  1. 1

    Drag to aim

    The bow sways. Read the wind indicator and lead your moving targets.

  2. 2

    Release to shoot

    You get five arrows per level. Let go at just the right moment.

  3. 3

    Meet the goal

    Reach the score or the level’s goal to clear it. Fewer misses earn more stars.

FAQ

Questions?

Contact us
Is it free?

Yes, it is free to play. Remove Ads, a Starter Pack and coin packs are available as in-app purchases.

Are there ads?

Interstitial ads appear only after a level clear. Rewarded ads, such as +1 arrow or double coins, are always optional.

Can I play offline?

Yes. Progress is saved on your device. Ads and purchases need a connection.

Does it end with the story?

After the story, the challenge goes on with stronger winds. Train five skills learned from the people you meet with coins.

Which languages are supported?

Ten: English, Korean, Japanese, Thai, Vietnamese, Indonesian, French, German, Italian and Spanish.

Is it on iPhone?

Right now it is available on Google Play for Android.
