A FEW LITTLE DETAILS

A few things you might wonder

Can I try it for free?+

You can make three free images per day, watching one ad for each image. Free results include a watermark. Styles and restoration share the same daily allowance. With passes, you can create without ads or watermarks.

What kind of photo works best?+

Choose a bright photo with a clearly visible face. Each style may have its own photo tips, so check the guidance in the app. Only use photos you have the right to use.

Can I restore old photos?+

In the latest app version, open Restore and choose In color or Clean up. When photographing a print, keep it flat, avoid glare and fill the frame with the photo. AI estimates colors and details, so results may differ from the original. Styles and restoration share the same daily free allowance and passes.

Where can I find my creations?+

Save an image to your device or share it from the result screen. Sign in to revisit finished images in My works.

How is my photo processed?+

After you agree in the app, your selected photo is sent through Pikmade’s server to OpenAI to create your image. Account information such as your email is not sent with it. Read the Privacy Policy for details.
