Pipelody

Puzzle

Link the colors.
Travel the world.

Connect matching dots with pipes and fill every tile to win. A pipe-connecting puzzle that takes you through 30 cities, from Paris to Tokyo.

AndroidGoogle Play

Story

Fill each board with same-color pipes, then ride the pipe map to the next city.

Paris, Tokyo, New York, London, Seoul… every city has its own sky and skyline, and every board you clear adds another landmark to your passport.

Features

Easy to start,
deeper as you go.

01

A puzzle that plays music

Every tile your pipe grows raises a piano note up the scale.

02

Three stars, fewer moves

Solve with fewer moves to earn more stars. Stars never block your progress.

03

Travel 30 cities

Each board moves you one step along the map, and the World Tour continues with new boards after a full lap.

04

Landmarks for your passport

Collect 150 landmarks like the Eiffel Tower, Tokyo Tower and the Statue of Liberty.

05

900+ puzzles

Boards grow bigger, some are on the clock, and Undo and Hint are there when you get stuck.

How to play

One-line rules.

  1. 1

    Connect matching colors

    Drag your finger to link dots of the same color with a pipe.

  2. 2

    Fill every tile

    Pipes cannot cross, and every tile on the board must be filled.

  3. 3

    On to the next city

    Each clear moves you along the map as you collect landmark cards and city stamps.

FAQ

Contact us
Is it free?

Yes, it is free to play. Remove Ads, coins and item bundles are in-app purchases. Stars, landmarks and stamps are never sold.

Are there ads?

An interstitial ad may appear every few clears. Rewarded ads are always optional.

Can I play offline?

Yes. Progress is saved on your device. Ads and purchases need a connection.

Which languages are supported?

Seven: English, Korean, Japanese, Spanish, Portuguese, German and French.

Is it on iPhone?

Right now it is available on Google Play for Android.
