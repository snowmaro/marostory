Light Every Room

Logic puzzle

Place lanterns.
Light every room.

Light up every tile with lanterns, and the room glows to life as its animal tenant is caught by surprise. A logic puzzle you solve without guessing.

AndroidGoogle Play

Story

Night has fallen on the old boarding house on the hill. Every room is dark, and someone is hiding in the shadows.

Each room you light makes the house at night a little brighter, and one by one you meet the animal tenants who were hiding.

Features

One lantern at a time,
one room at a time.

01

Light up every tile

A lantern shines left, right, up and down until a wall stops its light. Lanterns must never shine on each other.

02

Lights on! Who’s hiding?

Light every tile in a room and the lights come on, catching its animal tenant by surprise.

03

Fill the whole house with light

Five floors and thirty rooms. Each room you light makes the house at night a little brighter.

04

Mirrors and glass bend the light

Cracked floors, glass cabinets and mirrors change how the light works on every new floor.

05

240 puzzles, 30 tenants

Solve 240 puzzles and collect all 30 cozy tenants in the gallery.

How to play

Three simple rules.

  1. 1

    Place lanterns

    Tap a tile to place a lantern. Its light reaches left, right, up and down until a wall stops it.

  2. 2

    Read the numbers

    A number tells you how many lanterns sit in the 8 tiles around it, diagonals included.

  3. 3

    Light every tile

    Light every tile without lanterns shining on each other to clear the room. Short lessons teach each rule first.

FAQ

Questions?

Contact us
Is it free?

Yes, it is free to play. It includes ads, and Remove Ads, lantern designs and hearts are available as in-app purchases.

What if I get stuck?

A hint places one correct lantern for you. Every puzzle can be solved with logic alone, so you never have to guess.

Is there something new every day?

Tonight’s Room brings a fresh puzzle every day, along with daily gifts, the firefly jar and the weekly Lantern Festival.

Can I play offline?

Yes, you can play the puzzles offline.

Which languages are supported?

English, Korean, Japanese and German.

Is it on iPhone?

Right now it is available on Google Play for Android.
