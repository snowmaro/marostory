Sound familiar?

Turning an announcement or report into a video means opening an editor, planning scenes and adding captions and narration by hand. Even a short video takes a lot of work.

So we made this

Paste your text and hit ACTION. An AI director writes the storyboard and fills in the scenes, playback starts right away, and you can edit with plain words, then save an MP4 or share a link.

Features

Paste. ACTION.
Done.

01

Paste to start

Announcements, reports, patch notes — paste anything. Pick a template and just fill in the blanks.

02

Playback in seconds

Earlier scenes start playing while AI writes the rest. Close the window and the server keeps building.

03

Edit with words, then export

Ask for changes like "make it 30 seconds", then save an MP4 or share a single link.

04

Eight moods, three ratios

Switch themes to change backgrounds, transitions and emphasis. 16:9, 9:16 and 1:1 re-layout with one tap.

05

Numbers from your source

Every number in the video shows which line of your text it came from, with the formula for calculated values.

Which languages are supported?

TEXTAKE is currently available in Korean.

Does it cost anything?

TEXTAKE is free to use, with no payment. Sign up and verify your email to get 20 credits (about four 60-second videos), and earn more by watching ads or redeeming coupons.

How do I get more credits?

Watch an ad to the end for 3 credits (up to 10 times a day, used within 30 days). Enter coupon codes on My Page. If AI fails while creating, the credits come back automatically.

Do I need to install anything?

No. It runs in your web browser, including on phones. Sign up with Google or email.

Can I publish AI videos as they are?

The storyboard and narration are made by generative AI, so please review before publishing. Exported MP4s include information that they were made with AI.
