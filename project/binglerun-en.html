BingleRun

One-button runner

Just five more minutes…
and you got sucked into the feed!

A one-button runner where you tap to jump, run all four edges of the screen and turn your phone at every corner. Escape the Algorithm’s feed.

AndroidGoogle Play

Story

"Just five more minutes…" and you got sucked into a short-video feed. There is no exit, and the overly polite "Algorithm" keeps playing the next video.

Run the four edges of the screen from the Stone Age to the Industrial Revolution, from a raft to a rocket, through the Past Times Channel and the Vehicle Channel, and escape the feed.

Features

One button.
You turn the phone.

01

The screen edge is the ground

Hit a corner and you leap onto the next wall. Turn your phone and that wall becomes the floor again.

02

Season 1: Past Times Channel

The Stone Age, Ancient Egypt, Rome, the Vikings, Wuxia, Edo Japan and more. Every era changes the map, monsters, gimmicks and your outfit.

03

Season 2: Vehicle Channel

Rafts, bicycles, balloons, biplanes, submarines and a rocket, where one tap flips gravity so you run on the ceiling.

04

A boss for every chapter

When the boss stops to catch its breath, tap on the glowing pad and strike back. The Steam Giant waits at the finale.

05

Story told like shorts

Swipe-through story cuts open each chapter, and live comments flood the screen while you run.

How to play

One tap is all you need.

  1. 1

    Tap to jump

    Clear obstacles and enemies and collect likes. Every jump is graded from PERFECT to MISS.

  2. 2

    Corners take you up the wall

    Hit a corner and you leap onto the next wall and keep running.

  3. 3

    "Turn it!" means turn the phone

    Rotate your phone and the new wall becomes the floor again. You can also play without turning.

FAQ

Questions?

Contact us
Is it free?

Yes, it is free to play. Remove Ads and like packs are available as in-app purchases.

Are there ads?

Interstitial ads may appear between stages. Rewarded ads for reviving, double likes or a shop refresh are always optional.

Do I have to turn my phone?

No. You can play without turning. Turning makes the new wall the floor again so running feels easier.

Where is my progress saved?

Your progress and gear are saved on your device.

Which languages are supported?

Seven: English, Korean, Japanese, Spanish, Portuguese, German and French.

Is it on iPhone?

Right now it is available on Google Play for Android.
