SaiNote

Gift money & relationship notes

Remember the gifts
you give and receive.

A relationship notebook that keeps the money you give and receive at weddings, funerals and first-birthday parties, organized by person.

iOSApp StoreAndroidGoogle Play

Available in Korea · Korean only

Sound familiar?

"How much did I give at their wedding?" In Korea it is customary to return gift money in kind, but memories fade over time. Notes on your phone are never there when you need them.

So we made this

Record by person and occasion, and SaiNote totals what you have given and received for each person. When the moment comes, just search their name.

Features

Record it. Remember it.
Look back on it.

01

Record in seconds

Tap "I gave" or "I received" on Home. Continue from a recent record to add another with the same person and occasion.

02

"Who was it?" search

Search by name, occasion or memo, and see everything you have given and received with that person on one card.

03

Your records by month

Browse records month by month, and filter to only what you gave or only what you received.

04

Look back on your year

Insights show this year’s totals, the monthly flow, and records by occasion and relationship.

05

Back up only if you want

Start without signing in. Back up to an account for free whenever you like, and see the same records on another device.

How it works

Three steps to a record.

  1. 1

    Pick a person

    Choose someone you recorded recently or add someone new. You can also pick from your phone contacts.

  2. 2

    Add the occasion and amount

    Choose the occasion, date and amount. Quick buttons fill common amounts in one tap.

  3. 3

    Search their name later

    Every record and total with that person is waiting on one screen.

FAQ

Questions?

Contact us
Which languages and regions?

SaiNote is currently available in Korean, on the Korean App Store and on Google Play.

Is it free?

Yes. There are no in-app purchases, and a banner ad may appear at the bottom of the screen.

Do I need to sign in?

No. You can start right away without an account, and your records stay on your device.

What happens when I change phones?

You can back up to an account for free at any time, then sign in on the new device to see the same records.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Manage > Account and backup > Delete account. You can keep using the app without an account afterwards.
