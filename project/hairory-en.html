Hairory

Hair salon journal

Every salon visit,
saved with a photo.

A personal hair journal that keeps your photos together with what was done that day, ready to pull up before your next salon visit.

iOSApp StoreAndroidGoogle Play

Available in Korea · Korean only

Sound familiar?

Explaining the cut you loved, or what you want changed, is hard every single time. The photo is buried in your camera roll, and you cannot remember the treatment or the price.

So we made this

Hairory keeps the photo, date, treatment, salon, price, rating and notes together on one card, so you can show it at your next visit.

Features

Your past looks,
one card each.

01

Flip through your looks

Browse recent records like a stack of cards on Home and pull up the one you loved.

02

Everything on one screen

See the cover photo, treatment details, what you liked and what to ask for next time, together.

03

Photos with the details

Add up to five photos per record, with the first as the cover. You can also save just the date and treatment.

04

Quick search

Search dates, treatments, salons and notes at once, sorted newest or oldest first.

05

Filter what you need

Narrow records by treatment type, whether they have photos, and how satisfied you were.

How it works

Here is how to save a look.

  1. 1

    Choose photos

    Pick from your album or take one now. You can skip photos, too.

  2. 2

    Add the treatment and salon

    Choose the date and treatment type. Salon, stylist, price and rating are optional.

  3. 3

    Note what to remember

    Write what you liked, what to ask for next time and how to care for it.

Before your next salon visit,
save today’s look.

FAQ

Questions?

Contact us
Which languages and regions?

Hairory is currently available in Korean, on the Korean App Store and on Google Play.

Is it free?

Yes. There are no ads and no in-app purchases.

Do I need an account?

No. You can use it right away without signing up.

Where are my photos stored?

Records and the app’s photo copies stay only on your device. Nothing is sent to a server, and your original album photos are never changed or deleted.

How do I delete records?

Delete one from the menu on its detail screen, or delete everything at once in Settings.
