Ticktown Lifts

Management sim · Puzzle

You never drive the elevators.
You write their rules.

A pixel-art elevator puzzle where you write the rules that melt the rush-hour queue. Set the rules and the elevators follow them.

AndroidGoogle Play

Story

Welcome to Ticktown, a city that runs on time. You are the new elevator operator.

Finish probation at Ticktown HQ, then take on a new building’s rush hour every month for 30 months: offices, malls, hotels and hospitals, all the way to the city’s landmark tower.

Features

The result depends
only on your design.

01

Rules for every car

Served floors, where empty cars wait, who to pick up first, express runs. Set them and each car follows.

02

Read today’s demand

Arrival rushes, lunch crowds, going-home waves. The same rules always give the same day, so planning pays off.

03

Probation: one rule a day

At Ticktown HQ, a coach teaches you one new rule each day.

04

30 buildings, 30 months

A new building every month. Slow doors, small cars, fast cars: each one needs a new plan.

05

A new Daily Tower

A new building every day, the same for everyone. Compare your result with the planner bot, or try Endless mode.

How to play

How a shift works.

  1. 1

    Write the rules

    Set served floors, idle spots, pick-up priority and rule cards that switch on only when their condition is true.

  2. 2

    Start the shift

    When the rush hits, the cars follow your rules. Pause and edit any time.

  3. 3

    Keep waits under 90 seconds

    If anyone waits 90 seconds, the shift fails. Shorter waits earn more stars.

Are there ads or in-app purchases?

No. Buy once and play everything, with no ads, no in-app purchases and no sign-up.

What if I get stuck?

Rewind 30 seconds after a failed shift, or load an expert design on the rules screen, once per shift for free.

Can I continue on a new phone?

Progress is backed up automatically to Google Play Games, so it follows you to a new phone or a reinstall.

Can I play offline?

Yes, it plays offline.

Which languages are supported?

Seven: English, Korean, Japanese, Spanish, Portuguese, German and French.

Is it on iPhone?
