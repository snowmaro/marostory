Routiney

Routines & habits

Check off your routines.
See your consistency.

A simple routine app that reminds you at the times you choose and keeps a light record of what you did today.

iOSApp StoreAndroidGoogle Play

Sound familiar?

Routines are easier to start than to keep. Forget to check in and the streak breaks, and one missed day can make all your progress feel lost.

So we made this

Routiney reminds you on time and stacks your completions by week, month and year so your consistency stays visible. A day off never counts as a failure.

Features

Just what you need,
nothing more.

01

One tap to complete

Tap a routine card on Home to check it off, and see how many you have done today at a glance.

02

Completion history

See weekly, monthly and yearly history for each routine, with completed, partial and skipped days marked.

03

Routines you repeat all day

For things like drinking water, set a start and end time, an interval and a daily target, then fill it one check at a time.

04

Complete from the details

Check off "Done today" or "One check" right from the history screen.

05

Reminders on your schedule

For once-a-day routines like taking medicine or journaling, pick a time and the weekdays to be reminded.

How it works

Three steps is all it takes.

  1. 1

    Create a routine

    Pick a time and weekdays for a single check, or an interval and daily target for repeated checks.

  2. 2

    Check it off

    A reminder arrives at your time. One tap on Home and it is done.

  3. 3

    Skip today if you need to

    On a tough day, choose Skip today. It does not count as a failure and only pauses that day’s reminders.

FAQ

Questions?

Contact us
Is it free?

Yes. There are no in-app purchases, and a product banner may appear at the bottom of Home.

Do I need to sign in?

No. Use it right away without an account. Your records are saved on your device.

Do my records move to a new phone?

Routiney stores records only on your device and does not sync to the cloud. Deleting the app deletes your records.

What does Skip today do?

The day is not counted as a failure and only its remaining reminders stop. Pick up again on the next scheduled day.

Which languages and themes?

Korean and English, with light, dark and system themes. The app screens shown here are in Korean.
