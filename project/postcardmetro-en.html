Postcard Metro

Strategy puzzle

Draw a subway
on a postcard.

A calm subway strategy puzzle where you draw lines on paper maps of real cities and carry passengers home.

AndroidGoogle Play

Story

You are the city’s transit designer. You do not drive the trains; you design the lines.

From Budapest to Tokyo, stations and passengers keep appearing on postcard-style paper maps of six real cities. Your only enemy is overcrowding.

Features

Draw lines,
connect the city.

01

Drag station to station

Draw a line and trains start running. Passengers wait wearing the shape of the station they want.

02

Survive night and rush hour

Night, morning rush, day, evening rush. In stages, survive the rhythm of the day and open new districts.

03

Pick a reward every week

Choose a new line, a train, a car or a tunnel, and grow your network.

04

Six real cities on paper maps

Budapest, Pittsburgh, Seoul, Tokyo, Hong Kong and Amsterdam. Earn stars to unlock the next city.

05

Tunnel across the river

Real rivers and harbors shape every map. Crossing water takes tunnels.

How to play

How to run your metro.

  1. 1

    Connect stations

    Drag from station to station to draw a line, and trains start running.

  2. 2

    Plan the transfers

    Passengers show the shape of the station they want (○ △ □). Make sure every trip can get there.

  3. 3

    Beat the crowds

    If a station stays overcrowded too long, the run ends. Use weekly rewards to grow your network.

FAQ

Questions?

Contact us
Is it free?

Yes, it is free to play. Remove Ads is available as an in-app purchase.

Are there ads?

No ads during a run. An interstitial may appear when you leave a result screen, and rewarded ads are always optional.

Can I close the app mid-run?

Yes. Your run is saved, so you can pick up where you left off.

Are there other modes?

Normal and Endless let you grow your network as far as you like, and the Daily Challenge gives everyone the same map.

Which languages are supported?

Ten: English, Korean, Japanese, Thai, Vietnamese, Indonesian, French, German, Italian and Spanish.

Is it on iPhone?

Right now it is available on Google Play for Android.
